About This Platform

Emblem Print Shop is an extraction and research platform built for scholarly investigation of alchemical emblems. Using machine vision (GroundingDINO + SAM) and Claude-based re-identification, we've extracted 7,097+ visual motifs from 13+ historical sources, aligned them with source texts, and made them discoverable through rich browsing interfaces.

This work integrates Maier's Atalanta Fugiens, Cramer's Emblemata Sacra, Rosarium Philosophorum, Splendor Solis, Khunrath's Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae, Fludd's Mosaicall Philosophy, and others.

Platform Features

  • Vision-based re-identification: Machine-labeled elements verified with Claude Opus + diagnostic rubric. 121 animal crops corrected.
  • Junk filtering: Heuristic detection of text pages and full-scene crops; automatically excluded from gallery.
  • Text-visual linking: 56 emblems cross-referenced with related chapters in source texts. Read alongside visual analysis.
  • Concept indexing: 140 alchemical concepts mapped across emblems and chapters. Discover by theme, not just by image.
  • Rich metadata: Tags, categories, project affiliations, confidence scores, and scholarly citations.
  • Research tools: Search, filter, compare, edit. Export-ready JSON catalogs for downstream analysis.
