Eighthly, Auctor de Trium Verborum, the author of
The Three Words gives in his writings a peculiar method
to govern the Heat or the Fire, saying
: " When the Sun is
in Aries, he indicates the First Heat, or Grade of the
Firie,
which
is weak because the heat
is under the Rule of the
Water, but when the Sun
is in Leo, then
it indicates the
Second Grade, which
is hotter because the great coldness
of the Water being under the Rule of the Air.
In the Sign
of Saggitarius is the Third Grade, this being not of a burning
heat, and under the Rule or Order of Rest and Pause.
